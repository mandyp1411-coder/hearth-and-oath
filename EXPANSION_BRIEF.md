# Hearth & Oath: a deeper life

## Refined implementation prompt

Expand the existing Hearth & Oath prototype into a replayable high-fantasy life simulator whose central experience is living a remembered life, rather than selecting disconnected RPG bonuses. Preserve its parchment-and-forest visual identity, lightweight browser runtime, local saves, authored continuity, and existing life stories.

Add an explicit annual layer inspired by life simulators: let the player choose priorities and a living standard, review likely income, expenses, and commitments, and live toward their next birthday. Advance through understandable intervals; stop for significant decisions instead of skipping deadlines, relationships, hunger, or full moons. Annual events must draw on the actual character, seeded world, career, household, companions, relationships, knowledge, and previous decisions. Include a yearbook showing what changed and why. Keep the existing day-based activities available.

Deepen civilian careers, wellbeing, housing, friendships, courtship, family life, obligations, and personal ambitions. Use differing costs and opportunities to make humble, comfortable, and noble lives feel distinct while leaving viable routes open. Give players positive, difficult, and ordinary decisions; avoid a universal morality score and automatic NPC knowledge. Choices should create later consequences and fresh roleplay opportunities.

Replace the generic portrait with visibly configurable fantasy avatars. Provide live previews and expression through ancestry, skin, facial features, hairstyle, eyes, build, clothing, colors, accessories, scars, and tattoos. Include temperament, beliefs, and pronouns as roleplay choices. Appearance supports expression and changes with age and transformations; it must not create an attractiveness ranking or reduce a character's value.

Add a usable equipment and crafting economy, more tactical options and encounter variation, and lycanthropy with a visible lunar forecast, control, preparation, refuge, and consequences. Keep vampirism and lycanthropy mutually exclusive, preserve existing professions and history, and make cures and difficult choices playable.

Deliver complete working systems, integrated interface views, sufficient authored event variety, meaningful automated and browser verification, a refreshed standalone offline HTML, compatible old saves, and an update to the existing private Site. Avoid external AI services, installation requirements for players, dead-end progression, purely decorative gameplay statistics, and giant feature lists without functioning interactions.

## Acceptance

- A v1 life, including an active event or combat turn, resumes without losing people, knowledge, obligations, resources, or journal entries.
- A player can live consecutive years with different priorities, see accurate time and costs, face repeatable seeded but varied dilemmas, and review the resulting yearbook.
- An annual plan stops for choices and deadlines; supernatural needs remain understandable and recoverable.
- Careers, housing, friendships, courtship/family, and ambitions change through visible player decisions.
- Avatar controls visibly change the portrait at creation and later; aging and transformations remain recognizable.
- Gear and tactical abilities change encounters without permanently stacking bonuses or granting rewards twice.
- The same supernatural framework supports a vampire or werewolf life, secrecy, preparation, and a cure.
- Existing stories and meaningful checks remain intact; desktop/mobile and offline packaging receive real verification.
