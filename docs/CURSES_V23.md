# Curses in Hearth & Oath v0.23

Curses belong to the character’s life. They do not replace earned skills, a profession, relationships, inventory or the world’s history. Characters must be adults to acquire these transformations, and only one major curse can be active at once.

## The wound and the hunger

Follow a sound after sunset, or encounter a feral vampire in an expedition. Suffering damage in a vampire encounter can transmit a seeded blood lineage. The depicted enemy carries the same lineage as the transmitted bite. A bite starts a three-day infection. During that window, one sheltered day and 12 gold can cleanse it. The twilight patron offers a separate voluntary route into the Velvet Sepulchre.

| Original blood lineage | Memory and appearance | Learned blood gift |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Velvet Sepulchre | Royal tombs, exquisite manners, appetite beneath beauty | Weaken a foe and regain magic |
| Ashen Choir | Plague singers, ash and stolen final breaths | Ward the party and cleanse bleeding |
| Gloamwing | The oldest cistern, keen hearing, restless thirst | A piercing ambush and marked openings |
| Blackroot Communion | A murdered grove, dark veins and a buried pulse | Drain vitality and weaken a foe |

Each blood gift spends 6 MP and adds 4 hunger. Feral enemies have corresponding distinct battle roles: weakening, warding, targeting magic reserves, or draining health. These lineages and their lore are original to this game.

Hunger grows with elapsed days. Gloamwing blood thirsts faster and gains less relief from animals. Hunger above 85 accumulates starvation, bodily harm, stress and humanity loss. At sustained extreme hunger, an involuntary frenzy forces a decision; resisting can kill an exhausted body. Assaulting a named adult can kill that actual person, with family grief and a local report. In wilderness or a dungeon, a city resident or city watch is not invented.

Animal sustenance costs time without harming a person. An informed named donor may agree to paid sessions, refuse, become unavailable or withdraw. A standing arrangement is optional: it still attends each session, pays its fee and respects place and interval limits. Failure interrupts it. Humane feeding supports humanity; predation lowers it. Remembering a known person can recover a little humanity but does not feed the body.

Well-fed blood grants charm in conversation and presence in encounters. Starvation makes presence and willpower worse while sharpening predatory strength. Composed, starved, predatory and feral paintings follow these values. A vampire does not die of ordinary old age; starvation, daylight, wounds and execution remain lethal. After transformation, a full cure requires the existing healer rite and actual herbs.

## The moon and the beast

A moon-cursed beast can transmit the curse in combat. The moonwise ranger also offers initiation. New curses begin at control 12, with no earned mastery. A saved older invitation promising control 35 retains that promise.

Full moons fall every 28 days on cycle day 14. A prepared and available refuge contains the beast without harming outsiders. Preparation and weekly practice give small, capped control gains. Acceptance grants no instant power: it improves the mastery earned through repeated safe moons. Reliable practiced restraint requires control 85 and mastery 30.

Isolation injures the body. Failed resistance is worse. Releasing an untamed beast among settlement households can kill a recorded adult, attract silver-bearing hunters and break an oath. Wilderness contains no unseen settlement witnesses, but running as the beast still injures the character. Fatal transformation injuries end the life. Witnessing companions remember the night.

The transformed portrait follows the ancestry, actual form and adult or elder stage. The original mortal portrait returns on an ordinary day. A moonbound cure requires the existing healer route, gold and herbs.

## Discovery and law

| Region | Hunter order | Local curse policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bellwater | Lantern Inquest | Hearing and supervision |
| Saltreach | Salt Watch | Expulsion |
| Highstone | Silver Compact | Capital warrant |
| Blackgrove | Thorn Wardens | Supervised shelter unless assault is proved |
| Rainward | Ashen Vigil | Hearing and supervision |
| Ashplain | Cinder Tribunal | Capital warrant |

Orders follow recorded local exposure. They do not learn private deeds in every region. A warrant waits until the character is actually in the settlement; it cannot arrive at a remote dungeon door. Hunter defeat leads to capture and a special hearing rather than ordinary adventure recovery. Current donor or refuge records can support a stay, but cannot erase a recorded serious assault. Execution ends the life; a living heir may continue through the existing legacy system.

Prison has paid animal-blood provisions, free emergency sustenance and a requested barred moon chamber. Disclosing the need informs the local jailer. These routes prevent care from being the only visible answer to hunger; they do not acquit an existing crime.

## The portrait book

The update ships 32 generated whole atlases with 512 stage paintings: 24 ancestry and presentation vampire atlases, four wolf atlases and four Beastfolk supplements. All eight ancestries have vampire and wolf coverage; Beastfolk keep recognizable canine, feline, bear, horned, long-eared or mustelid anatomy. Supplement crops use measured native bounds around ears, horns and complete busts. Gray rendering removes slight source tints.

These are complete paintings. Hair and beards are never pasted onto another face. The new transformation identity remains consistent down its stage sequence, but an exact painted match to every legacy mortal face is not claimed. Beastfolk anatomy takes priority over a separate illustration for every blood-lineage combination. Some alternate cells are retained as assets rather than offered as selectable portraits.

While transformed, the portrait book explains why the condition controls the displayed painting. It does not offer a cosmetic choice that silently fails to change the visible face. Your saved mortal portrait and heritage remain intact.
