# v0.8 family, education, and relationship depth

The v0.7 audit found that schools behaved too much like interchangeable skill purchases, relationship decisions barely acknowledged a person's own values, and succession began mainly with infants whose development followed the same household routines. This expansion connects actual learning, known wishes, existing childhoods, and inherited identity.

## What now happens in play

- Four school routes each offer two distinct supervised projects: oral histories and a safe crossing; a healing garden and consent around ancestral accounts; a ward experiment and a restricted index; tool repairs and fair measures. Two actual attended sessions open these projects. Completing their three to six days of work leads to a decision about evidence, care, or practical use. The chosen result develops real skills and course progress, leaves teacher and participant memories, and can support an honest prepared examination. Each project is available once per school year.
- From age twelve, a pupil can discuss inquiry, public service, or practical learning. Choosing a direction grants no skill; subsequent completed projects practice its skill. It never assigns a combat class.
- Named teachers retire at seventy and hand the same school to a seeded successor. The earlier identity remains in a retirement record and existing correspondence. Active teacher conversations and pending examinations finish before a handoff. The world teacher roster continues across a player's succession; it does not become a new young teacher merely because the pupil changes.
- A two-day values discussion reveals a person's actual tastes and convictions. Relationship views show known shared preferences and differences. Respectful differences earn a small explained bond change; dismissing someone's wishes damages trust and leaves a boundary memory. There is no attractiveness or soulmate score, and a disagreement does not make courtship impossible. A new courtship can pause to discuss values instead of automatically turning a friendship into romance.
- Guardians can introduce young people around six, twelve, or sixteen. Each has their own name, appearance, birthday, preferences, prior attended learning, named guardian, and recorded orphan or shelter circumstance. Meeting takes two days; listening takes two more. A child can consent or ask to keep their current home. Only a consented offer can proceed to three actual preparation days and a thirty-gold home cost. A partial preparation pauses for the real calendar, can continue, or can be cancelled without a fee or invented child.
- Refusal allows respectful learning support, at most once per thirty days, without adoption, a new parent, or an inheritance claim. Single parents can adopt. An existing partner is not silently assigned co-parenthood by their bond score.
- Actual children can present supervised school projects after two attended sessions. The parent spends four days supporting inquiry, service, or the child's own preferred pursuit. The child develops their own skills and traits; the parent gains no copied skill, class, or certificate. Later family scenes remember the project and an adopted child's earlier guardian and adjustment.
- Funded community learning halls reduce real academy fees and child session fees. Funded apprentice halls support additional actual practical project learning. A building or enrollment alone does not grant a qualification.
- Succession preserves the selected child's original birthday, appearance, tastes, personal learning, and the same calendar. Actual adopted siblings keep their individual portraits and preferences too. The household's community works, known world people, career property, dungeon records, and claimed caches survive; personal commitments and supernatural preparation belong to the new person's actual day.

## Saved-game contract

The save revision remains five. Family depth is optional prospective content: `life.relationshipVersion`, each person's `compatibility`, `education.depthVersion`, and `lineage.adoption`. Existing names, geography, resources, children, learned skills, memories, ongoing work, examinations, and original choice effects remain exact.

An imported pending examination or teacher correspondence uses `education.nextDepthVersion` to wait before starting the new teacher/project layer. A completed discussion is never invented during migration: known preferences and new conversation histories start empty. Teacher names already implied by an older save are recorded rather than rerolled. Old infant adoption and household school arrangements remain available; an old pending academy decision retains its original fee.

Adoption preparation uses `lineage.adoption.preparation` with an actual stage, candidate identity, and completed-day count. Its handlers own time and fees so a birthday, health need, or death cannot produce a free instantaneous child. Candidate names are monotonic within the household, including after earlier young people become adults. A cancelled commitment retains the young person's wishes.

## Evidence

`scripts/playtest-v8-family.mjs` played four distinct public-API lives, each from age zero to actual retirement at thirty, without time, money, skill, or item grants. The final run recorded 499 operations. All four routes completed their two distinct projects and earned an actual prepared qualification. Three young people consented, at six, twelve, and sixteen, and each became the actual successor with exact personal identity and learned skills. A twelve-year-old declined the offer and received learning support while remaining in their own home. These are four lives, not twelve or more because reruns are separate evidence.

Summaries are in [playtest/v8/family.json](playtest/v8/family.json). Complete parent and successor action records are in `artifacts/playtest/v8-family/` for the audit archive. Ten focused regression tests cover known compatibility, school requirements and repeat limits, teacher identity and pending correspondence, old pending examination effects, all three adoption ages, refusal, partial preparation and cancellation, personal child project learning, co-parent consent, and support cooldowns. The existing household, lineage, and annual-life suites also pass.

The older children's guardians are named background figures rather than fully simulated new contacts. Project scenarios are authored and seeded within the four school routes. This adds consequences and continuity to learning; it does not claim an unlimited education or family simulation.
