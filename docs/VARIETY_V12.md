# Hearth & Oath 0.12 — portraits and varied lives

The head portrait remains a set of gritty engraved raster plates. This release adds 32 adult face plates, sixteen haircuts, and sixteen headwear or mask plates to the original atlases. There are now 33 hairstyle choices, illustrated male and female likenesses for all eight ancestries, and different human face variants. Facial hair, complexion, eye color, scars, markings, and accessories remain separately editable. Seven subdued patinas affect headwear and masks.

Masks are independent of headwear. A character can wear a hood with a lower face mask or a blindfold, for example. Closed masks conceal the face and facial hair; open helmets reveal the face. A registered opening in the frayed hood keeps the eyes visible. The cropped head portrait never reads armor, weapons, inventory bonuses, class, wealth, or combat statistics.

The creator has feature locks and a **Roll this portrait** command. This keeps the name, pronouns, world seed, ancestry, origin, household, and locked features. **Cast the bones** rolls identity and circumstances as well as the unlocked portrait. Names now come from the larger world name generator. Old body fields remain hidden and preserved in saves.

New lives give companions and family their own seeded identities. Parents and siblings inherit ancestry, complexion, and possible hair or eye colors; they do not inherit the player's helmet, mask, haircut, scars, or markings. Shelter guardians are independently generated people. New teachers, replacement teachers, and townspeople also retain their own illustrated likenesses. Presentation, adult adornments, and visual details follow age and work. Ancestry and physical traits never select moral alignment. Companion pronouns agree with existing story references. Contact views retain the same stored portrait rather than falling back to a contact-slot face.

Eight ordinary conversation chapters form a separately seeded eight-year deck per contact in new lives. Wage records, a hoped-for journey, an inherited token, an uncertain grave, unspecified armed service, a debt letter, an excluded feast guest, and an unsigned ritual page create different discussions. Replies have specific remembered outcomes and bounded learning. Pressuring someone for a favor damages that relationship; no reward is invented for the demand.

Six new road meetings join the existing seven: a courier, pilgrims, a witnessed grave robbery, a fortune reader, a damaged wagon, and a shrine repair fund. Choices spend actual time or coin, earn stated wages, record bounded discoveries, or change standing with a named witness. Recent harmless encounters are omitted from the next selection. A saved meeting stores its exact illustrated person; loading or reopening does not reroll it. Aid pauses with its remaining day and resumes without another fee or duplicate reward.

New appearance fields are optional in older canonical portraits. Existing saved histories, family identities, pending conversations, battles, routes, and decisions remain valid. New road content begins on subsequent leg checks, and new household generation applies to new lives. Inventory and profession mechanics remain independent of cosmetic selections.

Validation: 606 automated checks pass. Coverage includes canonical older saves, full campaign and dynasty paths, stable seeded portraits, family inheritance, role and age constraints, feature locks and actual creator handlers, all new road types, once-only costs and learning, delayed aid, recent-scene exclusion, and saved conversation replies. The standalone HTML was rebuilt; its bundled script parses and all eight portrait atlases are embedded. Raster composition checks inspected new hair and face registration, open and closed helmets, and hood/mask combinations. Browser layout and audio playback were not inspected in the managed container.

## Art provenance and production directions

New bitmap assets were generated with the imagegen skill and built-in ImageGen tool, using the existing portrait atlases for registration and the user's ink fantasy references for texture. Final WebP assets are under `dist/art/portraits/`.

- `faces-v12.webp`: sixteen different human and female nonhuman adults. Dark etched ink, bone highlights, realistic anatomy; bald, no adornments, generic dark collar, black background. Four equal columns and four rows, no lettering or gutters. Left-facing three-quarter profile. Eye at approximately 43%/35%, nose at 20%/47%, mouth at 29%/60%, ear at 76%/44%. Two rows of new rugged, fine, soft, and gaunt human anatomy; female elf, dwarf, halfling, orc, goblin, tiefling and wolf; an alternate male wolf.
- `kin-v12.webp`: another male and female pair for elf, dwarf, halfling, orc, goblin, tiefling, and wolf, plus a weathered human male and female. Same neutral engraved style, bald heads, pose, scale, collar, and registration.
- `hair-v12.webp`: isolated transparent hair only. Brush crop, brow fringe, broken bangs, wavy forelock, widow's peak, matted locks, rope braids, fishtail, shag, tonsure, tight curls, half knot, mohawk, locks across one eye, pageboy, and war braids. Neutral silver/charcoal so game tints can apply. Crown near the cell top and front hairline around 19–29%; no heads, skin, lettering, or gutters. A subsequent image edit shortened the first crop and removed painted scalp from the two shaved-side styles so complexion remains visible underneath.
- `covers-v12.webp`: isolated transparent medieval headwear and masks, with worn engraved iron, bone, leather, and cloth. Sallet, nasal helm, bascinet, closed visor, leather cap, wizard hat, copper mask, chain veil, frayed hood, bandana, rune blindfold, horned helm, raven skull mask, funeral veil, lower iron mask, and wicker cage hood. A subsequent image edit shortened and raised the wizard brim and removed the opaque upper opening of the lower half mask, preserving the surrounding fourteen cells.

All new atlases use four-by-four cells. Original atlases remain in use, preserving earlier choices and age stages. The original title illustration and the existing contextual soundtrack remain in place.
