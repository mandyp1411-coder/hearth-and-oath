# Mature portrait expansion

The eight accepted native atlases add **512 separate portraits for ages 40–54**, with 64 paintings for each of Human, Elf, Dwarf, Halfling, Orc, Goblin, Tiefling and Beastfolk. These are standalone portraits, not invented links to a younger character's face. The separate lifecycle expansion supplies linked identities; the application may use these additional portraits for compatible mature choices and stable phenotype-based fallbacks.

All paintings use the existing grounded medieval graphite treatment: practical wool, linen and worn leather, charcoal backgrounds, restrained ancestry features, natural faces and mature lines. No faces were composited or recolored programmatically. The source paintings retain slight graphite warmth, so the application's shared portrait rendering must enforce full grayscale on every surface. The AVIF files preserve the generated source's native dimensions and colors.

Each accepted image contains an observed 8 × 8 grid at 1254 × 1254 pixels. Boundaries are not assumed to be equally spaced: measured row boundaries and per-row column boundaries are recorded in `mature-observed-grids.json`. A three-pixel inset excludes the dark gutters. The catalog uses those measured rectangles directly. All 512 crops are within their decoded images, unique and nonblank; the smallest measured luminance standard deviation is 26.674.

The Beastfolk sheet covers all 44 known presentation, morphology, coat and species cohorts from the existing adult library. It contains 51 observed cohorts in total, including additional variations. One requested bear cell was painted as a feline and is registered as a feline. A genuine feminine dark bear elsewhere in the sheet preserves the intended known-cohort coverage. Coat and hair deviations are also registered rather than copied from the generation plan. Visibly bearded faces in several requested androgynous rows are registered with masculine presentation. Portrait presentation does not set identity or pronouns.

Six initial generations copied a lifecycle reference's smaller grid or younger ages and were rejected. They are not packaged or counted. The selected replacement generations use the successful new mature Human sheet as their style and grid reference. Every selected generation used the built-in imagegen tool. Full exact prompts and reference paths are in `mature-prompts.json`; selected source and encoded hashes are in `mature-registration.json`.

Run `node scripts/build-mature-portraits-v40.mjs` to recreate `dist/js/mature-portraits-v40.js` from the observed metadata. It exports `MATURE_PORTRAIT_ATLASES_V40`; each entry has `stage: 'mature'` and `modern: true`. Native asset sizes total 4,450,362 bytes. Existing portrait, save and family IDs are untouched by this asset module.
