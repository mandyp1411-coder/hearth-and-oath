# Earned-life balance regression gate (roadmap 7)

This is a reusable, bounded comparison gate, not a numeric rebalance, a population win rate, an optimal combat solver, or certification that the whole game is balanced. No gameplay, difficulty multiplier, gear value, maze door, fog rule, reward or recovery value changes here.

## Three distinct evidence classes

1. **Synthetic invariants:** `tests/second-wind-balance.test.js` and `tests/earned-balance-gate.test.js` isolate command legality, finite resource exchange and the harness itself. Their invented combat fields are never counted as earned lives.
2. **Action-earned encounter cohorts:** three historical age-18 Bard saves from the completed Second Wind experiment are checksum-anchored under `tests/fixtures/earned-balance/root-*.json.gz`. Their generated legal traits, ordinary paid work, mentorship, twelve-gold/three-day training and travel/recovery history are retained. Primary root A is extended through real offered actions into active Ranger (weapon), Elementalist (caster), Guardian (defender) and Bard (support) forms. Retained Bard training is explicitly recorded; these are cross-calling forms, not four independently randomized clean-class populations. Low and actual level-4 mid snapshots, legal solo/Orrin/three-companion selections, four encounters and two fixed policies make 192 primary cases. Actual weapon level-3/4/5 neighbors cover the free-encounter and maze tier thresholds. Two other trait roots provide targeted support checks. Up to 48 targeted cases include 12 clearly labelled perilous/level-8 counterfactual clones; these are NOT earned level-8 characters.
3. **Resource-carrying expeditions:** four offered `adventure_descend` entries buy a real tent, ration and available potions at the actual market. HP, MP, gold, supplies, living costs, days, kit nights, interruptions and battle outcomes carry through every public transition. Exploration consults only the current room and previously visited doors. It does not use the hidden goal, hazard locations or shortest full-maze path. A bounded route may finish, withdraw through its real entrance stair, or suffer a real defeat. An unfinished withdrawal is not dungeon completion.

The root files contain generated game people only. They reuse existing evidence rather than rerunning childhood. Their original setup was produced by `node scripts/experiment-second-wind.mjs earn` at source `99e667b`, building on v0.43.0. See [Second Wind method and findings](second-wind.md) for the original provenance and limitations. That earlier experiment remains the numerical baseline for self-refund removal; this broader gate starts after that fix and the attended active-party camp policy.

## Design intent before tuning

- Routine roads should have an approachable option for a rested earned adult. A new fixed-policy defeat is a review signal, not a reason to grant universal victory.
- Ruins and procedural tiers may demand resources or withdrawal. A tier/stat jump alone is not a demonstrated difficulty cliff.
- Named guardians may be genuinely dangerous solo. Weapon, spell, defense and support should retain different useful exchanges, rather than converge on one free sustain loop.
- Self Second Wind cannot refund its cost indefinitely. Explicit Defend recovery turns, basic Bard verse and two-actor MP transfers remain deliberate, observable tradeoffs. The mutual ally-transfer interaction is recorded, not silently nerfed.
- Preserve the user's saved 11×11, 121-room maze, connected reciprocal doors, fog, finite caches, prior-room retreat and actual stair exits. No gate authorizes topology simplification.

Hard failures are invalid/checksum-edited saves, unearned fixture edits, illegal commands, mutation of public inputs, nonfinite/negative/overcap resources, mismatched comparison inputs, incomplete required scope or changed maze topology. Preparation and explicit validation replay setup extensions from the checksum-anchored roots through the actual public methods, then save a content-bound provenance certificate. Before combat, the certificate, roots, complete fixture contents and actual save validity are checked. Replay with the declared historical preparation source, not a future candidate: harmless wording changes must not invalidate old earned evidence. A forged 500-HP fixture cannot pass just by fabricating a plausible hash chain.

Soft thresholds ask for design review: an outcome change, more than max(3 actions, 25%) action drift, 20 percentage points of hero HP, 25 points of total-party MP, any changed potion expenditure, and expedition day drift beyond max(2 days, 25%) or changed kit/ration/potion use. These thresholds tolerate incidental one-hit/one-turn changes but surface consequential attrition. They are not target win rates, automatic nerf knobs, or exact-output golden tests. A case-specific review acceptance needs a substantive reason tied to design intent and matched resources. Update these review thresholds deliberately when the gate's purpose changes, not to hide an uninvestigated regression.

## Finite commands

Run from the repository root with Node 20 or later. No dependencies or full test suite required.

- Inspect the frozen matrix: `node scripts/check-earned-balance.mjs plan`.
- Extend the already-earned roots once: `node --max-old-space-size=512 scripts/check-earned-balance.mjs prepare --revision=YOUR_SOURCE_COMMIT`. Each form has a 300-public-transition setup budget. Missing stages are saved as NOT RUN and fail coverage rather than receiving invented stats. A development-only smoke setup can use `--only=support --fixtures=/tmp/your-copied-fixture-directory`; it cannot pass the full gate.
- Verify/replay all checked-in snapshots using their historical preparation checkout with --modules=/absolute/preparation/dist/js when needed: `node --max-old-space-size=512 scripts/check-earned-balance.mjs validate`.
- Synthetic contracts: `node --test --test-concurrency=1 tests/earned-balance-gate.test.js tests/second-wind-balance.test.js`.
- Baseline: `node --max-old-space-size=512 scripts/check-earned-balance.mjs run --modules=/absolute/baseline/dist/js --label=baseline --revision=BASELINE_COMMIT --out=artifacts/earned-balance/baseline.json`.
- Replay five representative exact command traces: `node --max-old-space-size=512 scripts/check-earned-balance.mjs verify-replay --revision=TESTED_SOURCE_COMMIT --report=artifacts/earned-balance/baseline.json --out=artifacts/earned-balance/replay-control.json`.
- Candidate, using the exact same fixture directory: `node --max-old-space-size=512 scripts/check-earned-balance.mjs run --label=candidate --revision=CANDIDATE_COMMIT --out=artifacts/earned-balance/candidate.json`.
- Compare: `node scripts/check-earned-balance.mjs compare --baseline=artifacts/earned-balance/baseline.json --candidate=artifacts/earned-balance/candidate.json --out=artifacts/earned-balance/comparison.json`.

Use separate sequential runs, not two simultaneous engine graphs or the entire historical suite. All encounters stop by 120 party commands. Each of four expeditions stops by 120 successful walks and 300 total post-entry maze/battle/event/outcome commands, reserving a known return path. Setup, policies, exact commands, actual traits/calling/skills/gear/party/difficulty, opening/ending reserves and costs are exported as JSON. Unresolved action-limit probes are not wins. No stochastic sampling means no claimed win rate.

An intentional change can be reviewed with `compare --accepted=/path/reviewed-cases.json`; that file is an array of `{ "id": "exact case id", "reason": "specific design rationale and evidence" }`. This acknowledges the observed change only, not universal balance. `--encounters-only` produces an explicitly incomplete diagnostic run and cannot pass the full comparison gate.

## Evidence for this implementation

Executed on the isolated ID7 evidence-only branch against the gameplay graph at `abf06b2`. Its module graph was byte-identical at that execution point. This is not a claim that any later combined release graph is identical or independently rerun:

- All 12 fixture setups replayed successfully. Primary low snapshots are actually level 2; mid snapshots level 4; weapon boundary snapshots level 3 and 5. Largest extension is 104 public transitions, below the 300-transition limit. Other trait roots retain genuinely earned levels 2 and 1.
- 240 deterministic encounter cases completed: 192 primary, 36 other earned spot checks and 12 explicitly counterfactual perilous/level-8 cases. Attack diagnostic observed 102 victories/18 defeats; resource-aware observed 106/14. No case reached its action bound (max 29/27 actions). These are counts of fixed policy cases, NOT win rates, and they do not justify blanket numerical changes.
- Four natural-entry expeditions each really returned through the entrance after 118 walks, visiting 61 of 121 rooms, clearing eight battles and collecting eight caches. All are **unfinished withdrawals**, not objective completions. They share the primary seed's maze geometry, not four independent maze samples. Weapon solo used 186 total commands and 37 attended days; caster/Orrin 175 and 29; defender/three 177 and 29; support/three 191 and 27. Each stayed below 120 walks/300 commands, kept valid saves and preserved the exact topology hash. Real travel/setup fees, entry ration and camp nights were paid. All heroes ended at zero Focus; active companions retained their actual reserves. Level-ups earned during the route can raise ending maximum HP/MP.
- Zero natural expedition interruptions occurred. These four routes do not establish birthday/interrupted-rest behavior; the separate existing camp/movement regression suites own that coverage.
- Five representative exact-command replays reproduced final battle hashes. The 10 synthetic harness tests plus 21 existing Second Wind contracts passed: **31/31**. This includes stale/illegal casts, finite self cost, preserved Defend/basic verse and mutual ally transfer.
- A zero-delta comparison control passed with no review findings. No separate candidate graph was independently run because there is no gameplay delta; the control compares the same genuine baseline report with itself. Negative synthetic tests establish that mismatched inputs, incomplete scope and material outcome changes do not silently pass.

The complete current baseline and exact commands are retained compactly in [earned-baseline.json.gz](earned-baseline.json.gz). [earned-baseline-summary.json](earned-baseline-summary.json) contains the small resource/outcome summary; the checked-in fixture index and provenance certificate bind the full earned snapshots. Future numeric changes must run baseline and candidate graphs separately with the exact same fixtures and review changed outcomes/attrition. This gate validates the stated evidence and tradeoffs only, not all classes, all traits, all difficulties, all dungeon completions, economy balance, or human enjoyment.

The repository-wide historical aggregate, browser/touch behavior and a second independent candidate matrix were not run for this source-only validation task.


For a later combined-release smoke check without rerunning all 240 cases/four expeditions, use `node --max-old-space-size=512 scripts/check-earned-balance.mjs verify-replay --modules=/absolute/combined-checkout/dist/js --revision=COMBINED_COMMIT --report=docs/balance/earned-baseline.json.gz --out=artifacts/earned-balance/combined-replay.json`. Expected success is five matching final battle hashes. A mismatch requires investigation; five passing representatives are not a full combined-release matrix or expedition rerun. The output separately records baseline revision and actually tested replay revision/module directory.
