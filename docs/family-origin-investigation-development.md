# The Fold in the Page: family-origin investigation

## Scope and source base

Roadmap 18 implements one optional, finite family-record investigation from age twelve onward. Work began in an isolated branch at published source `4c8f38c`, not on main. The owner-independent `objective-evidence.js` helper is imported unchanged from `2745b16`; no unfinished faction state or runtime is imported. Generated standalone output is not part of this source branch until explicitly built and verified.

The old `familyOriginInterest` flag only creates an invitation. Neither the flag, reading the invitation, nor migration accepts work, awards evidence, proves a parent survives, or creates an obligation. Declining is never a failed task. The game keeps one authoritative family-origin run, not a second task ledger.

## Source-confirmed constraints

- `household.js` only wrote the interest flag in the origins dilemma. Birth-parent records can be unknown, missing or deceased with no person ID; they are not living contacts.
- Shrine-foundling parentage requires both parent identities to stay unknown. No search mutates those statuses.
- `caregiver()` may fall through to a different surviving relative. This story captures the exact actual guardian and does not use that fallback during continuation.
- Family visits use the recorded home and Homes room. Actual archivists work in School & Archive; Shrine Keepers exist only where an actual service is offered.
- Existing personal stories are adult-only. This small independent module supports adolescence without weakening their age gates.
- Narrative edition adapters replace unknown events and outcomes. The investigation preserves only its own validated snapshots and literal results, following the narrow Winter Levy/Causeway pattern.

## Exact childhood anchors

Freeze the supported subset of `timeline.storybook.flags`, the relevant physical-item flag, and an explicitly recorded `hero.lifeOrigin.id`. Do not rewrite any earlier scene, choice, record, name, flag, item or outcome.

- `birthThread`: registered, hidden, burned. A registered packet and a household keepsake are different sources. A burned thread never returns.
- `birthVisitor`: bird, name, closed. A bird is physical evidence only if `items.boneBird` supports possession. A sound like a place is not an authenticated person.
- `birthName`: household, margin, blank. An unused naming entry is not a parent identity. A blank line contains no recovered second name.
- `cradleSong`: kept, changed, silenced. Changed words never reappear by convenience. The silenced branch does not force a performance or fabricate remembered lyrics.
- `firstWord`: recorded, game, corrected. An uncertain sound is not retrospectively translated into proof of ancestry.
- An explicit life origin can provide a concrete existing community motif: communal bread, shared makers’ work, seeds or the shrine’s preserved bundle. Legacy saves without those facts get a limited household-copy variant, not a retroactive heirloom.

## Three short authored scenes

### What was kept

The actual caregiver sets the supported object or household account between you: “I kept what I could. That is not the same as knowing where everything began.”

Ask about the object/song/mark, ask what the recorded names actually tell us, put the papers aside, or respectfully stop. A live interview takes one attended day and no fee. An unavailable guardian leaves only the documented account; no conversation, bond or new personal memory is claimed. A dead guardian is not replaced by a sibling or a new service worker.

Deceased parents retain their exact recorded names and deaths. Missing parents remain missing. Unrecorded parents remain unrecorded. The search is for a truthful account, not a promised reunion.

### The mark beside the names

Meet the exact captured archivist in School & Archive. They open the page at its worn fold: “Let us start with what is actually on the page.”

A real one-day inspection can reveal why the bundle was kept, a shared makers’/care mark, an ordinary communal song, or the limited meaning of an unused naming line. The discoveries are specific to the saved anchor. They do not turn a material association into biological identity or present survival.

An intact source can reference a surviving counterfoil and identify a real current custodian. A partial, blank, burned, uncertain or inaccessible source can supply no verified lead. Register unavailability is recorded as such, never as a completed inspection. An unavailable named archivist is not silently replaced.

### What you carry forward

1. Follow the verified surviving lead: visit the current exact-ID custodian at their actual shrine. They are not claimed as a witness to the hero’s birth. After one actual day they may share a bounded account or refuse. “I can look at what is in my keeping. I cannot promise to tell you whose hands first held it.” A willing account gives a concrete community connection and its limits, not a new parent contact.
2. Keep both accounts: preserve remembered upbringing beside uncertain documentary evidence. “The tune may be familiar and the handwriting strange; neither has to silence the other.” This is a complete and satisfying ending, not failed content.
3. Close the search: fold the papers along their old crease. The people who raised the hero remain real; the unanswered question need not become tomorrow’s work. Keep all acquired evidence. No failure or penalty.

There is no recurring deadline, automatic quest interruption, combat, payment, faction-rank gate, class assignment or guarantee of discovery. Pausing retains the exact run. Stopping is terminal for this one investigation and cannot farm rewards.

## State and migration

`familyOrigins` is optional for old saves. Its envelope owns version, introduction day, invitation disposition, run, pending proposal and attended project. Initialization is idempotent and never autoaccepts.

An accepted run stores stable ID/content version, generation and acceptance date/age, status and next step, source location, frozen family/parentage/member records, frozen childhood anchor, exact cast references with capture dates, typed evidence, separately attributed findings, decisions/history, ending and completion date.

Null-person-ID birth-parent records receive local stable subject keys in the frozen snapshot; they do not acquire NPC identities. Exact guardian and archivist references are captured on acceptance. An optional lead is captured only when an actual completed inspection verifies current custody.

Old interest flags create offers only. Pending old birth, family, school and personal-story decisions remain unchanged. Historical participants may subsequently die or retire without invalidating the saved account. New generations start without an inherited active investigation. Retirement/death records accepted unfinished work honestly and clears pending attendance.

## Evidence is not truth

The shared helper supplies a bounded vocabulary, duplicate prevention and one-time completion. This owner validates exact token meaning, actors, objects, locations, chronology, acquisition choices and dependencies.

Tokens are guardian account/unavailability, register inspection/unavailability, counterfoil reference, current custodian verification, willing consent/refusal, and actual custodian account. A verified token means the attributable action occurred. A finding separately says documented, attributed or unresolved. Presence is not consent. Only a willing consent token can support a heard-account ending.

Selected proposal terms are kept once per scene/question, with the latest unworked selection replacing its earlier attempt. Repeated cancellation cannot overflow the bounded choices or evict a choice supporting already acquired evidence.

No evidence is automatically shared with faction missions or inferred from map arrival, eligible actions or opening a proposal. No reward is paid by a generic helper. One practiced Scholarship follows the actual register inspection; it does not repeat. A living guardian gains a small once-only memory/bond change for a completed interview. No terminal ending needs to invent an additional reward.

## Availability and interruption

Exact individual IDs never alias successors. Actual mortality, membership availability, current provider identity and actual room presence outrank frozen historical descriptions. Retirement does not establish living whereabouts. No ninety-day small-favor exception applies.

Opening any scene costs zero. The one-day attended step persists through birthday, hunger, full-moon and deadline interruptions; zero attendance acquires no interview or inspection proof. Continuation spends remaining time only. Actual household living costs remain separate from the zero provider fee, and dedicated search days do not earn annual wages. Recheck the participant after time passes. Death, retirement, displacement or site loss cannot yield personal testimony.

A future successor-custody feature would require an explicit named logged handoff and could never transfer personal testimony or consent. This implementation does not invent that handoff.
