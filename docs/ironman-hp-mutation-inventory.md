## Captured policy before opening turns

- `combat.js:createHumanDuel` is entered only for `watchhouse_duel` and first calls `validTournamentContext`. That validator binds the actual accepted card, current hero owner, paid preparation, exact host/opponent, watchhouse room/site/jurisdiction, available people and current legal/occupancy state. It captures `{nonlethal:true, solo:true}` before `beginTurn`/enemy processing. The actual constructed party is only the hero even if source companions are active. Imported saves also pass `validTournamentBattle`/`validTournamentContext`; a display label or boolean is insufficient.
- `engine.js:performEventOption`, `initiation_trial` → offered `combat`, creates an Ironman-only exact Paladin context after the current accepted choice: `{kind:'initiation',classId:'paladin',ownerId,locationId,oathType}`. `paladinTrialContext`/`validPaladinTrialContext` bind a living current adult warrior owner, pending Paladin specialization, actual authored oathType/oath text and usable current site. Entry also requires the offered enabled combat choice and 20 HP. `combat.js:createBattle` authenticates this context before capturing nonlethal rules/opening turns and retains the normal actual party. Imported saves revalidate context, source flags, owner and captured policy. Unmarked initiation, Necromancer and Assassin trials remain lethal, including assassin `lethalCommission`; sharing the `initiation` ID or a guarded-sounding name never creates an exemption. Standard's existing initiation call and serialized output have no new context or metadata.
- `yard-tournament.js` Gateyard Ribbon is expressly supervised/nonlethal but uses three event-score exchanges, not `createBattle`, `startBattle`, damage or combat HP. It creates no mortality policy or casualties. Ordinary day costs/natural mortality remain unchanged.
- `tactics-practice.js` creates its own fixed fictional `trainingSource`, with no live game input or Ironman metadata. Its `root_spoor` battle and actions remain memory-only; practice cannot add mortality to the live life. Closing/reloading discards it. Its existing serialized Standard golden remains unchanged.
- `combat-guidance.js:encounterOutlook` uses `createBattle(...,{openingTurns:false})`; it captures detached policy but advances neither live people nor storage. Command preview/detailed evidence also operate on copies.

All other actual battle entries remain lethal when opted into Ironman: engine forest/ruin expeditions; travel road beasts/bandits; ordinary exploration dungeon fights; campaign final covenant guardian; curse stranger/hunter fights; maze authored/generic/contract/signature guardians; wilderness country encounters; adventuring contracts/roams/descents; radiant descents; realm raids/conquest; RPG guardian/pursuit; regional political fights. `captureBattleMortality` uses the same `filter(recruited && active).slice(0,3).filter(alive !== false && hp > 0)` current-revision entry selection as real actor creation, excluding temporary actors. Both constructors then validate the captured participant set against their actual constructed non-ephemeral party before opening turns. Captured policy (`version`, `runId`, `lethal`, exact participants) is immutable in `resolveBattle`; recorded deaths can only accumulate, never disappear/change person. Malformed explicit mode metadata throws before opening combat instead of downgrading to Standard.

## Every in-battle HP write

`combat.js`:

- `makeHero` and companion profile construction copy/clamp initial resources only. No death from starting zero is fabricated; a new Ironman battle requires a living positive-HP hero. Downed/dead companions are excluded at entry.
- `beginTurn`: poison, bleed and burn decrements go through `setHealth`, which latches a first positive-to-zero transition immediately. Hero terminal prevents turn advancement. Companion death advances from the actual prior order. The poison prior-order correction is Ironman-only; Standard retains its historical ordering.
- `dealDamage`: primary damage and bramble retaliation both go through `setHealth`. A primary hit plus its existing retaliation are one strike; a simultaneous last-enemy zero never overrides hero terminal. Later calls after hero terminal or against a latched party casualty deal no damage.
- `useSkill`: Berserker 4-HP cost uses `setHealth`; healer/specialist heals, Druid self-heal, Bard rally and Necromancer drain self-heal use `restoreHealth`. No latched named actor, or any actor after hero terminal, can be healed.
- `useTactic`: every support Health restoration uses `restoreHealth`.
- `applyBattleCommand`: Feral 3-HP cost uses `setHealth`; bloodline drain, blood feeding and potion restoration use `restoreHealth`. Living target eligibility additionally respects the death latch. Capture/surrender direct enemy HP-zero writes remain explicitly living capture, not party death. They cannot target party members.
- `enemyTurn`: ordinary Thornheart regeneration and Leech healing use `restoreHealth`. Ordinary area waves stop before later recipients after hero terminal; no later victim status/enemy bookkeeping follows terminal. `processEnemyTurns`, `beginTurn`, `advanceTurn` and `finishIfNeeded` respect the exact terminal outcome.
- Accepted captured magic can be negative (existing battle validation allows gear.magic down to -200). `restoreHealth` therefore routes an actual negative restoration through `setHealth` for captured Ironman policy, including healer/Druid/advanced restorative paths; it does not clamp or alter Standard restoration arithmetic. The controlled accepted-stat regression asserts actual `validateSave` admission before a nominal healing command causes and latches hero zero.
- Constructor enemy scaling/growth writes only initial enemy Health/maxHealth before play. They are not named party casualties and need no mortality hook.

`techniques-v27.js:applyAdvancedTechnique`:

- Blood momentum (4 HP) and Iron fury (5 HP) use optional `loseHealth`; existing UI survival-cost eligibility remains unchanged. A terminal cost stops the command before later damage/recovery.
- All `heal` calls (Shared renewal, Purifying touch, Second wind) use optional guarded healing. Living party collection and the latch prevent named revival.
- Iron fury, Chain lightning and Hunter's volley stop their later damage callbacks at terminal. The common hit/status/ward helpers respect optional stop policy.
- Grave attendant's direct HP assignment activates only `ash_attendant`, the existing explicitly ephemeral construct; it never resolves to a lineage person and never enters the casualty floor.
- With no stop/cost/heal hook, all existing direct-call Standard behavior remains the original arithmetic and loop order. Combat supplies behavior-equivalent hooks for Standard, with no serialized extra fields.

`enemy-plans-v27.js:executeEnemyPlan`:

- Heal and all Drain/conditional-drain/drain-and-share restoration use optional guarded healing. A hero-killing strike cannot be followed by regeneration/recovery.
- Wave/burning-wave/poisoning-wave stop before later actual recipients at terminal; common strike respects the stop hook. Post-terminal enemy turn bookkeeping stops.
- Enemy-only regeneration remains ordinary combat behavior until hero terminal. Enemy death, captures and summons are deliberately not named party casualties.

## Captured-participant validation across engine settlement

The phase-2 engine applies companion deaths at accepted battle creation/command boundaries, updates actual person/lineage/RPG flags and ledger within one detached acceptance result, and settles terminal before rewards/recovery. Once applied, the dead participant remains a zero-HP captured actor despite no longer being active/recruited. Validation deliberately checks that actor's exact current lineage identity, not a newly rebuilt active roster. Real hero/roster IDs are classified independently of imported side/ephemeral flags; only the known ash construct is excluded. Already-dead current companions require matching applied casualty, zero captured actor and exact captured death for this same battle/day/generation; a dead person without that record cannot be smuggled back as a new casualty. Detached phase-1 latches may still have living unapplied records. The companion-only applied ledger independently requires all matching dead flags and dates. `killCurrentCompanionPerson` uses the exact current lineage ID, not `killNamedPerson`'s historical first-match local-ID lookup. Natural successors carry run identity/history and generate new companion people. Durable mortality transactions and app guards remain later phases. No full mode is yet safe to expose or publish.

## Every whole-life companion HP/membership writer

This inventories existing writers, not a claim that every path was exercised dynamically. No additional production module is changed in phase 2: consistent atomic death flags satisfy their existing eligibility rules.

- **Creation:** `engine.js:createGame` initializes the original companion resources/membership. `rpg-depth-v27.js:addCircle` creates only absent extra roster IDs, never overwriting a deceased companion. `initializeRpg` fills only missing stage records and derives `dead` from actual death. These are initialization paths, not resurrection services.
- **Accepted combat:** `engine.js:beginBattle` installs instance/context before exact-person application; `chooseBattleAction` applies each accepted command; `resolveBattle` verifies the completed result before application and ordinary settlement. Captured resource synchronization preserves the dead actor's zero HP. Victory increases combat experience only for living Ironman companions; Standard remains unchanged. Ordinary defeat recovery selects only recruited/active companions. Hero terminal returns before all resource/reward/recovery/encounter callbacks, including maze aftermath.
- **Ordinary rest:** engine `rest` and `camp_rest` write HP/MP only for recruited companions; applied casualties are unrecruited. `exploration.js` authored dungeon rest requires recruited/active and positive HP. `radiant-descent.js` camp uses its shared recruited/active/alive/positive-HP party selector. No doctor stabilization is added.
- **Field potions:** `field-treatment.js:applyFieldPotion` resolves a fresh quote at execution. `targetReason` rejects actual/RPG death, unavailable/away/departed membership, inactive/unrecruited companions, actors outside the actual first three, and nonpositive HP. A stale healing quote is not authority.
- **Field assistance:** `companion-field-assists.js:applyMazeFieldAssist` re-quotes helper/target state. `personReason` rejects actual/RPG death, unavailable/away/departed status, nonmembership in the actual first three active/recruited companions and nonpositive HP. The final HP write cannot select an applied casualty as helper or patient.
- **Travel/maze camp:** `travel-energy.js:restAtCamp` captures attending IDs, spends the actual day, then recomputes `campRecoveryView`; only still-attending living present positive-HP companions receive HP/MP. Party recovery defaults false (ordinary trail camp is hero-only); actual maze camp passes `party:true`. Death/departure during the attended day cannot turn the old quote into revival.
- **Party controls:** engine `recruitCompanion`/`toggleCompanion` recheck `companionActionReason`, rejecting actual/RPG death before writes. `rpg-depth-v27.js:applyRpgAction` first requires an actually offered enabled `rpgActions` row; that list excludes actual-dead companions before `rpg_meet_*` can recruit reserves. `finishProject` checks actual death before companion progression/service rewards; real departure clears active/recruited. `people-context.js:respectPersonalBoundaries`, conversation agency/censure and engine coercion only remove active membership.
- **Death/calendar:** `lineage.js:death` sets lineage/actual dates and actual dead/inactive/unrecruited flags and performs existing grief/family/journal consequences. The exact helper adds HP zero and RPG `dead`/no away-return date before its ledger append. `onLineageTime` skips dead records. Engine `spendDays` updates companion age only, using `npcAge` frozen at deathDay. `rpg-depth-v27.js:onRpgTime` handles actual-dead companions before away-return, enforcing `dead`/inactive, never healing/recruiting.
- **Growth:** engine `earnExperience` heals/grows only the hero. Companion `combatExperience` changes derived combat stats through `companion-combat-profile.js`, not actual companion HP/membership. A hero-level-up victory cannot refill a deceased companion. Death-frozen age avoids calendar identity drift.
- **Migration:** engine `migrateSave` validates/clones rather than reconstructing accepted companions. Roleplay initialization preserves existing lineage/stages; missing RPG stages derive dead status; extra-companion creation skips existing IDs. Invalid/dead-conflicting Ironman metadata rejects, never repairs into life. Phase-2 tests include ordinary time and actual validate/migrate roundtrips after application.
- **Natural succession:** `lineage.js:createSuccessor` carries the same `runId`, `startedDay` and companion-only ledger. Old people become historical ancestors with immutable IDs; fresh current-generation same-roster-ID companions intentionally start alive. Historical casualties are never re-applied by roster ID. Internal combat-terminal state cannot enter succession.

Terminal runs have no engine actions; public mutation/progression functions (including preferences, notes, map/portrait/appearance, party, gear and travel) are guarded before mutation. Accepted opening-terminal results also stop wrapper personality/preference/planning/narrative bookkeeping. Detached preview constructors remain pure. Store/session exclusion, durable deletion and export/resume fencing are not supplied by these engine guards.

## Phase-3 storage write and ownership inventory (closed source gate)

- `ironman-storage.js` is detached: strict run/marker codecs, raw empty-base inspection, explicit historical shared-mortality detection and review tokens only. It performs no storage/engine/DOM/lock side effect.
- `saves.js:commit` remains the original Standard mirror-first/envelope-last commit with rollback/exportable-unsaved behavior. Private Standard slots/backups/observed/validation drafts never contain virtual Ironman heads. Independent record/discovery corruption does not broadly disable valid Standard durability; recognized shared mortality still blocks destructive rewrite.
- `saves.js:persistRecord` writes and confirms only `hearth-and-oath:ironman-run:v1:<runId>`. The complete validated head and companion floor commit atomically; attachment metadata shares that record. New/recovered attachment is limited to an exact raw-empty Standard slot+backup token. No Ironman mutation writes shared envelope/mirror or unrelated backup bytes.
- `inspect`/`commitRun`/`reattach`/`retainOrDelete`/`recoverableHeads` run inside a privately captured actually entered exclusive lock and complete both-prefix discovery. Current revision/nonce/base guards stale/recreated owners. Conflicting claims remain blocked unchanged; only a readable observed occupied/token-mismatched base auto-revokes into a retained archive. Unreadable/unsupported bases block unchanged (or unconfirmed/frozen after an already confirmed head write), not an inferred orphan. Wrong-slot requests cannot archive a valid claim. Known imports identify retained authority, never overwrite it. Archive retains a full recoverable head; true deletion retains only a nonplayable floor record and forbids automatic same-run reimport in v1.
- `endRun` binds current hero/day/generation/floor and an already stored battle's captured instance/policy before marker creation. It writes/confirms only `hearth-and-oath:ironman-ended:v1:<runId>` first. `cleanupEnded` then removes/confirms only that exact run record and matching private copies; a confirmed existing marker permits retry despite a stale original token or unrelated corrupt discovery. Unexpected shared same-run full copies produce cleanup-pending, not destructive repair or a false byte-removal claim.
- `list`/`read`/`health` derive detached virtual views only; adoption/export require a synchronous locked `inspectIronman` consumer. Old unchanged markers do not evict unrelated Standard validation caches. Storage events/session references remain phase-4 work. Unobserved raw-token ABA is explicitly not detectable, but independent run state/floor/marker cannot roll backward through an ordinary Standard write.
- Passed whole files: `tests/ironman-store.test.js` (44 cases) and `tests/ironman-save-coordination.test.js` (35 cases). Fixtures use bounded published adult/actual migrateSave and controlled saved battles, plus synthetic storage/lock failures and race hooks; they do not claim earned lifetime balance, real-browser timing, app/UI protection or release QA. The 12-file source gate passed all 160 cases; historical phase-2 lineage failures remain separate debt, not relabeled here.

## Phase-4A session reference/acceptance inventory (authored, NOT RUN)

- `ironman-session.js` is DOM-free and performs no direct storage/engine command. It invokes the actual guarded store and injected synchronous adoption/matching-end callbacks. New `tests/ironman-session.test.js` owns controlled actual store+engine cases; no app/creator/helper changes or opt-in exposure are included.
- Private binding retains current source identity/fingerprint/token. A reserved immutable operation owns its exact candidate/source/original token separately from view epoch. Status and settlement snapshots contain metadata only. `invalidate` cancels stale presentation effects, never live/terminal/first-save retry obligations. Terminal takes priority, then unresolved candidate reconciliation, then idle probe; unchanged pre-command state cannot revive a companion or hero.
- Living adoption and export/practice consumption occur only in fresh synchronous locked consumers. Exact candidate permits one adoption; unchanged original token permits identical retry; different authority stays blocked. There is no engine replay, generic overwrite permission or silent Standard fallback. `releaseStandard` refuses unresolved/busy obligations, including uncertain first commits.
- Explicit lifecycle methods reserve archive/delete/reattach; only a fresh reviewed same-run lifecycle intent can supersede lifecycle uncertainty, never a gameplay/fatal/first-save obligation. Validate target/context before replacement. Reattach uses separate expected head proof and a second locked inspection; unrelated binding is not blindly marked ready after an await. A slotless existing-marker cleanup retry is allowed, not slotless ordinary load/commit.
- Stored-orphan live/create/import uses narrow `recoverCandidate`: separate reviewed attachment parameters, original operation/candidate/gameplay token retained, fresh locked inspection must prove exact candidate before adoption. Explicit `chooseAuthority` loads a genuinely newer saved head after a rejected stale action only when reviewed current token and full body both differ from original source; no-op revision cannot escape hero/companion intent. Neither path replays an engine command or relabels the old candidate.
- Confirmed hero marker disposes matching internal full candidate/source/binding and invokes external matching-run cleanup even after view invalidation. Cleanup-pending retains tiny marker/run metadata; runId-only retry relies on the actual store's existing-marker-first guard and cannot create a marker. The application must later clear its matching game/saved/import/recovery/practice references; this module alone does not prove that integration.
- `afterAdopt` is once-only, canceled on view invalidation, and never invoked for terminal outcome. Invalidation during adoption/effects or after protected consumption cannot be overwritten by a later ready callback. Exact source fingerprint prevents in-place mutation passing reference equality. Future app adapters must preserve synchronous Standard Boolean update semantics while moving gear/craft/trade/notes/maze/reply and focus/scroll success work into accepted callbacks. Full boundary and exact state table are in the implementation contract. Authoring is not execution, native QA or complete-mode acceptance.
