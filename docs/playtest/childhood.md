# Childhood, education, identity and correspondence audit

Six distinct lives start at actual age zero. They use different seeds, birthplaces, household means, family structures, ancestries, beliefs, preferences, temperaments, schooling and adult ambitions. The policy never changes age, day, money, skills or state fields. Each action uses the public engine, verifies the advertised option, proves its input stayed unchanged, validates the resulting save, and records decisions and journal changes. Baseline and final replays are the same six configurations; they count as six distinct lives, not twelve.

Full reproducible operation logs and final saves are in ignored `artifacts/playtest/{baseline,after}/childhood/`. Tracked summaries are `childhood-baseline.json` and `childhood-after.json`. Run `node scripts/playtest-childhood.mjs` or add `--after`. Unknown events and replies fail the journey instead of being silently skipped. The initial baseline exposed the mortality bug below; it was repaired before the sixth life could close validly.

## Findings and changes

1. **Critical mortality/calendar defect, reproduced through real play.** In Aster's age-up, physical mortality closed the life on world day 37595, but the age-up loop then opened `birth_adult_year`. The retired save became invalid. The calendar now stops immediately after elapsed time or follow-up processing closes a chapter. It never creates a post-death birthday or grants later annual income. A focused test covers the exact ordering; the complete scholar life then reaches a valid death.

2. **Meaningful civilian identity already works.** Bracken never chooses a combat path, keeps the blacksmith profession and reaches 70. Early schooling is optional; free independent reading and later practical qualifications remain real routes. This is a foundation to preserve, rather than forcing everyone into the campaign. No fabricated successful profession or training result was inserted into the run.

3. **Class initiation was too shallow.** Four baseline earned callings required genuine preparation and location, but the final decision was always a two-day accept/defer scene. New trials retain that accessible route and add an independent practice or an actual class-specific community-service promise. Service has its own six-day action, real sixty-day deadline, named mentor memories and trust consequences. Late service earns three trust instead of eight and preserves its missed deadline; independent practice costs five mentor trust and adds no extra deadline. The combat gifts remain the same and never stack. Paladin service also fulfills actual order service. Twelve distinct service descriptions preserve the class's theme.

4. **Work and personality lacked synergy.** Blacksmith, farmer, alchemist, innkeeper, miner, bard and cartographer work defaulted to a trade pursuit. All twelve careers now recognize an appropriate favored pursuit. Commission making recognizes the actual profession and making interest; finished committed work and masterworks have explicit completion contexts. Job selection, starting/continuing/canceling a batch and merely opening a masterwork grant no joy. The existing thirty-day enjoyment limit remains. Career and engine owners provide the actual completion signal.

5. **A professional life deserves relationship dialogue.** New vocation correspondence uses actual working days and delivered commissions. A character can explain their purpose to one person, ask what work has cost the relationship, or keep their work private and listen. These decisions create participant-specific memories. They do not assign a profession, hand out career experience or tell bystanders a secret. An already pending old conversation keeps its original replies.

6. **Family upbringing lacked concrete class pressure.** New noble invitations ask whether the character will represent a household, help a classmate or decline the family expectation. Poor households face a winter repair/time-for-learning decision. The tradeoffs affect actual household support, stress, happiness and, for the sponsored application, six gold when available. They preserve free education and never pick a child's combat class. Both are gated to new roleplay content; old stored pending dilemmas remain resolvable.

7. **Annual family scenes were disconnected from born-at-zero play.** The family audit owner supplied age-aware child, partner, elder and widow scenarios. They now appear on eligible third adult birthdays and change the actual person's traits, skills, relationship or memory. A child's lesson does not grant a parent a skill or school certificate. Optional content markers allow an imported pending birthday to resolve under its old rules before future roleplay content activates.

8. **Teachers needed individuality and calendar continuity.** New schools snapshot four seeded full teacher names and absolute birth dates. Their ages follow world days, including later generations. Existing schools without this roster retain their old teacher identity and thread history. A twenty-five-seed focused check produced more than twenty distinct names.

9. **Death-aware correspondence needed consistency.** Source review and a focused regression found that children lacked the living-contact check already used by relatives and companions. A deceased child now has no new conversation action while their historical thread remains readable. This particular correction was not discovered in the six childless audit biographies; the family cohort covers child mortality separately.

## Assessment and remaining design work

The connected simulation is a stronger prototype than the disconnected menus implied: poverty does not close education, qualifications come from attended work, friendships retain specific memories, advanced identity emerges from learned skills, and a whole civilian life can close without adventure. It is not yet a deep authored RPG merely because the seed changes names and geography. Six distinct lives cannot exhaust every combinatorial outcome.

The largest remaining roleplay weakness is character agency outside the player's action. Peers, tutors and mentors react to choices, but many do not originate sustained ambitions or competing requests. Long friendships deserve small returning story arcs with recognizably different preferences, grief, convictions and practical limits. Family-origin interest should eventually become a real research/search quest instead of remaining a remembered flag.

Many school dilemmas offer three reasonable outcomes with very small tradeoffs. More multi-year scholarship, patronage, disciplinary and apprenticeship arcs would connect upbringing to adult opportunities. This should expand consequences and recognizable people, rather than multiplying nearly identical lessons or hiding an attractiveness/morality score. A class-specific service promise helps, but an initiation could later become an actual expedition, investigation or social quest instead of an accepted narration of a trial.

The menu also has avoidable duplication: the old five-profession apprenticeship chooser competes with the twelve-profession career board; school-note review competes with formal attendance. Prefer one clear professional entry point and explicitly separate independent practice from qualifications. Keep old pending saves compatible while reducing redundant new-life UI choices.

Care, privacy, disagreement and ethical boundaries should remain available without every social choice automatically increasing trust. The new independent and household-expectation routes introduce visible costs, but future arcs should include conflicting sincere commitments, repaired mistakes and companions who choose their own boundaries. The most useful next additions connect existing calendars, households, work and local laws; adding more isolated meters would increase friction faster than roleplay depth.
