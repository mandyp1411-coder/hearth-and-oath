# A life from sixteen

Play [Hearth & Oath v0.71](https://hearth-and-oath-v1-a73f.nilled463.chatgpt.site/).
Allow about two hours for the first sitting. You may pause or stop.

Make a life from this field in the way that makes sense to you. Read the
choices and play as you normally would. There is no required career,
ambition or ending. If you become stuck, say what you were trying to do.

## Begin

Your observer will assign one of these age-sixteen lives:

- [The freeholder's start](common-freeholder-age16.json)
- [The tenant's start](bound-tenant-age16.json)

Download that JSON file. On GitHub, open the file and use **Download raw
file**; save the JSON itself. On the game's title screen choose **Import a saved
life**, open the downloaded file, select a slot marked **Empty**, then choose
**Import into the empty slot**. Your sixteenth birthday is still yours to
answer. Use a separate browser profile if all three slots already hold
lives you want to keep.

## While you play

**Life → Today** holds the current decision. The season and year controls let time pass;
answer any scene that interrupts them. **The Book** lets you read the life
so far. **Menu → Export life** downloads a copy you can resume later.

At the end of this life, **Continue the family story** leads to any living
children who can continue it. Choose **Continue as [their name]** and play
the next chapter. If there is no heir, tell your observer what happened.

If the sitting ends first, export and continue another time. Tell your
observer when a control is confusing or you want to stop. Play the choices
you want to make.
