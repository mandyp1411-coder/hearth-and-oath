## After the Winter Levy

The existing story and its original four outcomes remain unchanged. Its exact clerk and factor can now respond differently to a public correction, negotiated relief, paid silence, or private leverage. Their replies distinguish what they participated in from what they cannot know. You can ask a question, offer an opinion, change the subject, or leave; there is no new obligation or reward.

At your own home, an actually present adult partner or trusted current household member can hear your full account, learn only that you were involved, or respect your decision to keep it private. Household membership does not grant prior knowledge. These confidences are bounded receipts for this life, not inherited knowledge for a successor.

Seven days after completion, you may return to the original owner of the still-available earned service for a little ordinary company. These visits contain no errand, fee, service use, stat bonus, or repeatable reward. Saved replies and the actual spoken exchange remain literal in the Journal.
