# Hearth & Oath v0.57.1

All adult portraits now have identity-preserving later-age artwork. This release adds 390 native age atlases containing 24,672 registered portraits for 8,224 existing adult identities, alongside the 312 existing modern adult families. Masculine, feminine and androgynous portraits are covered for Human, Elf, Dwarf, Halfling, Orc, Goblin, Tiefling and Beastfolk ancestry.

The age bands are mature (40–54), older (55–64) and elder (65+), illustrated at approximately 45, 58 and 72. Faces keep their ancestry, complexion, species features and presentation. Explicit saved portrait choices and existing direct same-person age links remain stable.

The v0.57.0 gameplay fixes remain included: observational infancy, age-appropriate correspondence, varied family letters, immediate letter opening, removal of the character-creation Ironman rules block, and removal of the full-screen pending-save confirmation screen.

The catalogue is generated exclusively from accepted visual reviews and native raster verification. Original PNGs, exact prompts and generation references are retained. AVIF encoding preserves native dimensions at quality 85, effort 6, 4:4:4. The hosted package stores media once; its download recipe reconstructs the same complete standalone HTML.

Full portrait coverage evidence is in `verification/v0571-portrait-coverage.json`. Targeted catalogue, identity, gallery, creator, infancy, correspondence, pending-save and offline-download checks are recorded separately. Native-browser rendering, physical-device behavior and the previously incomplete full-game regression suite remain unverified.
