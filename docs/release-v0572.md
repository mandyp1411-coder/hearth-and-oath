Preparing Medicinal Herbs now opens a care review with the recipient, current time, supply and fee costs, supported benefits and a separate final commitment. The resulting receipt explains actual consumption, elapsed time and care effects. Treatment rules and balance are unchanged.

Personal-note drafts survive ordinary navigation and stay associated with their own life during the current page session. Drafts clear after successful persistence or explicit discard. Journal search distinguishes no matches from an empty notebook or quest list, with separate counts and Clear Search. Pronoun interface labels are capitalized consistently. Settings, saved-life selection and letter-to-Explore routes receive deliberate focus.

The v0.57.1 portrait catalogue, observational infancy, age-appropriate varied correspondence and removal of pending-save/loading screens remain included. No new art or save migration is introduced.
