# The Watchhouse Challenge · roadmap 33

## Scope

One finite adult local challenge card, not an invented knockout bracket or simulated round-robin. Three actual adult resident identities enter under the exact current Watch Captain at the actual Watchhouse. Soldier livelihood, earned Knight calling, Gateyard Ribbon and ordinary adventure remain separate.

The first invitation freezes the local identity/style offer. Acceptance records current ages and commits one actual preparation day. Birthdays and needs use the existing annual interruption budget; only attended time is credited. No entry fee, free healing or equipment is supplied. Household accrual remains separate. The controlled bouts add no calendar days.

Conversation service-person and meeting dictionaries are optional in existing saves. Acceptance initializes them lazily using the existing bounded recording pattern. It preflights the whole exact cast against the 512-entry capacities before changing the card or attending time, reserves their relationship rows, preserves existing identities/histories, and records only the actual current captain and residents. A full book that needs new rows leaves the invitation and uncommitted card in place with an honest reason; already retained participants can still fit a full book without replacing anyone.

Between bouts, a non-event card shows actual resources and results. The existing owned-potion action works normally: one potion, up to 30 HP. Suspend for Normal Care releases the blocking commitment so ordinary rest, treatment, shopping, equipment and travel retain their normal prices and time. Resume requires the original administrator, next living available entrant, settlement and sponsoring jurisdiction. No successor, invented entrant or walkover result replaces unavailable people. Closing or withdrawing adds no fee or time and does not settle unrelated old household charges.

## Combat and consequences

The competition constructor uses the real hero conversion and equipment, one named human actor, public canonical cycles, real turn processing and resource costs. No synthetic practice life, party assistance, summoned actors, monster species plans, bosses, secret phases, weaknesses or build-dependent scaling are used.

Version-one balanced styles are provisional pending bounded earned-life evidence:

- Guard reader: 32 HP, power 6, agility 4, armor 1; ward 5, then strike.
- Measured charger: 36 HP, power 6, agility 3, armor 1; charge 4, then a charged strike with no additional scripted modifier.
- Counter-step: 34 HP, power 5, agility 6, armor 0; remove up to 3 ward before striking, then strike and weaken the next attack by 2.

Gentle subtracts 4 HP and 1 power; perilous adds 4 HP and 1 power. The accepted difficulty/profile table is fixed; earned strength may outperform this local event. It never raises a foe in response to the hero's gear, level or companions.

A closed humane command policy applies identically to displayed offers, preview and execution. It checks actual class variants, not just generic skill/tactic IDs. Poison, burning/bleeding attacks, feeding/draining people, necromantic binding, captures, executions and summons are unavailable with an explanation. Permitted abilities retain their ordinary formulas/costs. Nineteen explicitly reviewed advanced abilities remain supported. Future unknown commands default to unavailable.

Zero bout HP is the marshal's stopping condition, not an NPC death. A strictly matched terminal tournament battle is settled before general adventure consequences. Defeat returns the hero with at least 1 life HP; remaining MP/potions persist. No three-day recovery, percentage fine, forced retirement, NPC death, wound diagnosis, captive, curse transmission, generic XP/loot, initiation, campaign proof or preference reward is applied. Normal personality effects of actual commands remain existing rules. All normal non-tournament consequence paths remain in place.

Three actual wins award one 24 Gold purse with zero XP/equipment, atomically with the terminal result and receipt. A loss remains recorded and removes purse eligibility. The hero can still continue or stop. The card never renews annually; reload/stale results cannot claim a second purse.

## History, continuity and UI

At most four participant snapshots and three result receipts are stored. Score derives from the receipts. Each result cites the exact person, profile, battle instance, day, round count, starting/ending combat HP/MP and potion count. Combat-end zero HP is distinguished from the living adjudication floor. Participant ages are recorded against their actual public age basis on acceptance and when their bout begins.

NPC16 retains referenced original identities. It pins only active authored scenes/battles, never an indefinitely paused office. Retirement is not reported as death. Actual recorded deaths take precedence. Custody/location/other-commitment interruptions suspend the card without bypassing their owner. Closing a life closes unfinished play; heir continuation does not inherit the parent's personal card or recognition.

The action boundary reconciles the card both before and after existing interruption processing. In particular, a late justice flush can create an arrest after a defended allegation, and birthday processing can claim the calendar after the initial reconciliation. The final idempotent pass pauses only the card; it does not answer or reorder the mandatory event, release custody, repeat attended preparation, or award a purse. Source inspection of the other late travel, health, lineage, sanctuary, fellowship, community, society and career flushes established no other equivalent returned-save defect. This is not a runtime clearance or a claim that a custody soft-lock had been reproduced.

A later in-person meeting with one actually fought rival can recall their exact win, loss or interrupted bout and the public local card. It requires a later actual day and that exact person's availability. The first opponent who defeated the hero is preferred, otherwise the last actually fought opponent. No pre-bout withdrawal invents a rivalry. One reply consumes the callback, without time, reward, stat/bond change or a new commitment. It is not an infinite rematch or a repeat prize source.

Training, Life's current commitment, and accepted Journal returns share the same card and authoritative actions. Watch Captain services own entry. The nonlethal battle result UI does not quote ordinary defeat fines/days or recommend forbidden companions. Finished cards remain available in Training without permanent Life clutter. The existing grayscale/dark presentation and shared interaction layout are reused.
